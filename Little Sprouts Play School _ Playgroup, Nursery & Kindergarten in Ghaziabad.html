A second home, built around children

We believe the early years are for curiosity, confidence and friendship. Our days are playful, but nothing is random: every activity builds language, motor skills, social skills or early thinking.

🧩

Play-based learning

Children explore letters, numbers and colours with blocks, art, stories and songs, not worksheets.

🤝

Caring teachers

Trained, patient educators and a low child-to-teacher ratio so every child feels seen.

🛡️

Safe and clean

Soft-edged furniture, child-safe materials, daily sanitising, CCTV and a verified pick-up process.

🌳

Outdoors and nature

A shaded play area and a small garden where children plant, water and watch things grow.

Programs for every stage

Age groups are guidelines. We place each child where they will be comfortable and challenged.

1.5 to 2.5 years

Playgroup

A gentle first step away from home.

  • Sensory play and music
  • First words and routines
  • Parent-friendly settling in
2.5 to 3.5 years

Nursery

Language, movement and sharing take off.

  • Rhymes, stories and phonics sounds
  • Colours, shapes and counting
  • Pre-writing strokes
3.5 to 4.5 years

Junior KG

Building independence and early skills.

  • Letters and number sense
  • Group projects and show-and-tell
  • Art, craft and dance
4.5 to 5.5 years

Senior KG

Ready for primary school, confidently.

  • Reading and writing readiness
  • Simple maths and problem solving
  • Responsibility and teamwork

A day at Little Sprouts

A predictable rhythm helps young children feel secure. A typical day looks like this.

  1. 8:30 am

  2. 9:15 am

  3. 10:00 am
    Snack and handwashing
  4. 10:30 am
    Learning centres: language, numbers, art
  5. 12:00 pm
    Outdoor play and garden time
  6. 1:00 pm
    Quiet time: story, puzzles, rest
  7. 2:30 pm
    Goodbye circle and pick-up

Admissions

Enrolling is simple. Admissions are open for the coming academic session.

  1. Visit us

    Tour the classrooms and meet the teachers.

  2. Apply

    Fill in the form and share your child’s birth certificate and photos.

  3. Meet and greet

    A relaxed play session. There is no test or interview for the child.

  4. Join

    Complete the fee formalities and settle in gradually with us.

Common questions

What age can my child start?

Children can join Playgroup from 1.5 years. Contact us if your child is slightly younger or older and we will suggest the best fit.

How do you help children settle in?

Parents can stay for short periods in the first week, and we shorten the day at first. Most children settle within a couple of weeks.

Is food provided?

Children bring a home-cooked snack and lunch box. We follow a nut-aware policy and note every child’s allergies.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the program and session. Please contact us for the current fee structure.
